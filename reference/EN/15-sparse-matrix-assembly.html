sparse() constructs the matrix but does not solve the system

          
After assembly, a sparse matrix behaves like an ordinary matrix: extract Kff, multiply by a vector or pass it to the standard solve operator. Octave recognizes sparse storage and selects appropriate numerical algorithms without requiring you to recreate a square array of zeros.
