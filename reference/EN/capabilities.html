MKE-F capabilities

MKE-F is an educational program for linear finite element analysis of planar trusses and frames.

Notation and limitations

Notation: X and Y are the global in-plane axes; θz is nodal rotation; N is axial force; V is shear force; M is bending moment. A free degree of freedom means that the translation or rotation is not prescribed by a support and is determined by analysis; it does not imply motion without elastic resistance.

Load conventions: no loads are specified in modal analysis. In transient analysis, type 10 is also interpreted as a one-step pulse, not a constant load; use type 13 for a constant action. Self-weight is not added automatically.

Scope: types 112 and 113 cannot be mixed in one model. Analysis is linear, with small displacements and a linear elastic material. Plasticity, geometric nonlinearity and damping are not included; the frame element neglects shear deformation. Distributed loads must be uniform and are supported only on frame elements in static analysis. Frame supports of types 2 and 3 restrain rotation: they are not conventional roller supports.

See Input file format for input records and Boundary conditions and loads for sign and load conventions.

MPC

Seven frame support masks, three unique truss combinations and homogeneous linear MPCs are available in all three analyses. The editor, node-on-axis helper, separate SPC/MPC forces and v3 export are implemented. Mz end releases are available in all analyses with JSON v4. Nonzero prescribed displacements and the full NAFEMS investigation remain separate work.
